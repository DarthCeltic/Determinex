# Determinex Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-04-16
**Maintainer:** Ryan Gurganious

---

## Permitted Use

Determinex is a local AI-assisted code generation and multi-model orchestration system,
free and open source under AGPLv3. There is no commercial tier and no separate license
required — personal, research, and commercial use are all permitted under the same terms.
You may use it for:

- Personal software development and experimentation
- Research and education
- Commercial and production use, including hosted services (AGPLv3 requires that if you
  distribute Determinex or run a modified version as a network service, you make the
  corresponding source available to users of that service — see [LICENSE](LICENSE))
- Security research in authorized environments (penetration testing with written authorization,
  CTF competitions, defensive tooling, vulnerability disclosure research)

---

## Prohibited Use

You may **not** use Determinex, its fine-tuned models, its training pipeline, or any output
produced by this system to:

1. **Attack financial systems** — unauthorized intrusion, manipulation, or disruption of
   banking, trading, payment, or financial infrastructure.

2. **Attack critical infrastructure** — power grids, water systems, hospitals, emergency
   services, transportation systems, telecommunications, or government systems.

3. **Develop malware** — ransomware, spyware, keyloggers, trojans, botnets, wipers,
   or any software designed to damage, surveil, or extort without authorization.

4. **Conduct unauthorized surveillance** — stalkerware, covert device monitoring,
   or mass collection of personal data without consent.

5. **Generate weapons** — code or systems for physical weapons, biological/chemical
   weapon synthesis assistance, or CBRN (chemical, biological, radiological, nuclear)
   weapons research.

6. **Circumvent authorization** — access systems, networks, accounts, or devices that
   you do not own or have explicit written permission to access.

7. **Produce illegal content** — anything that violates applicable law in your jurisdiction
   or in the jurisdiction where the system will operate.

8. **Deceive or defraud** — phishing infrastructure, fraudulent credential harvesting,
   fake identities at scale, or systems designed to deceive users into harm.

---

## AI-Generated Output

Code produced by Determinex is generated by AI models and compiled/syntax-checked for
correctness. It is **not** reviewed by a human before you receive it. You are responsible
for:

- Reviewing AI-generated code before deploying it in production
- Ensuring output does not contain security vulnerabilities before shipping
- Validating that the output does what you intended

The Determinex contributors make no warranty that generated code is correct, secure,
or fit for any purpose. Use is at your own risk.

---

## Security Research Exception

Authorized security research (penetration testing with written authorization, CTF
competitions, academic vulnerability research, defensive tooling) is permitted and
encouraged. "I was doing security research" is not a blanket authorization — you need
actual authorization from the system owner before testing against any system you do
not own.

---

## Enforcement

Violation of this policy terminates your right to use Determinex under AGPLv3.
Violations involving criminal activity will be reported to relevant authorities where
legally required or appropriate.
