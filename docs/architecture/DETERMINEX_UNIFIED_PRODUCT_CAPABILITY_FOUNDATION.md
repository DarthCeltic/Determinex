## Product Shape

Determinex is defined here as a unified verified software factory with five
surfaces:

1. Idea Lab
2. Repo Clinic
3. Maintenance Bay
4. Learning Studio
5. Proof / Operator Center

All five share one authority and evidence spine. Readiness is not execution,
request is not approval, approval is not verifier success, verifier success is
not training eligibility, and no source mutation is authorized from this
campaign.

## No Success Without Verifier

Creation cannot claim a working local app without build/test and smoke evidence.
Existing-repo repair cannot claim fixed without post-apply verifier evidence.
Maintenance cannot claim updated without compatibility verification. Learning
Studio cannot authorize source mutation or claim a repair.

## Cost And Setup

Local/open software claims require caveats. Hardware is not free, local models
need RAM/VRAM/disk, CPU-only can be slow, cloud APIs can cost money, and real
apps may still need hosting, domains, databases, app stores, paid APIs, payment
processors, project dependencies, and local toolchains.
