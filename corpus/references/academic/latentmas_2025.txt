Latent Collaboration in Multi-Agent Systems (LatentMAS)
Jiaru Zou, Ruizhong Qiu, Gaotang Li, Xiyuan Yang, Katherine Tieu, Pan Lu, Ke Shen,
Hanghang Tong, Yejin Choi, Jingrui He, James Zou, Mengdi Wang, Ling Yang
arXiv: 2511.20639 | ICML 2026 Spotlight
Submitted: November 25, 2025

Abstract:
LatentMAS enables purely latent collaboration among LLM agents in multi-agent systems
by replacing text-based inter-agent messages with last-layer hidden-state embeddings
stored in and transmitted via KV caches. After agent A1 completes its generation pass,
the system extracts KV caches from all L transformer layers as paired (K, V) tensors.
The successor agent A2 prepends those caches layer-wise, conditioning its generation
on A1's full internal state without text serialization. A linear alignment matrix W_a
is computed via ridge regression to prevent out-of-distribution activations.

Key results:
- 14.6% accuracy improvement over text-based baselines
- 4-4.3x inference speedup across nine benchmarks
- Training-free: no gradient updates required
- Validated on Qwen3 4B/8B/14B models on MBPP+ and HumanEval+

Relationship to Determinex Rosetta Stone:
LatentMAS operates on same-family models with matching hidden dimensionality.
Determinex Rosetta Stone bridges architecturally distinct models with different
embedding dimensions (e.g. Qwen2 3584-dim and Llama 4096-dim) using trained
nonlinear MLP encoder/decoder pairs with InfoNCE contrastive learning.
The Rosetta Stone requires a training phase; LatentMAS is training-free.
These are complementary approaches addressing overlapping but distinct problem spaces.
