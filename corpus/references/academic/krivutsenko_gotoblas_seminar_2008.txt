NOTE ON PROVENANCE — THE URL IS MISLEADING
The domain is libeigen.gitlab.io and the filename is matrixmult.pdf, which reads as an Eigen
project paper about Eigen's own GEMM. It is not. It is a TU Munich student seminar write-up
OF the Goto and van de Geijn paper, by a different author, hosted on that domain. Recorded
here explicitly because a corpus entry that inherits its identity from a URL is how a
citation quietly becomes wrong: anything downstream attributing this to the Eigen project,
or treating it as primary work on Eigen's kernels, would be citing a summary as a source.

RELATIONSHIP TO THE PRIMARY SOURCE
Secondary. It restates and explains the GotoBLAS approach — the layered gemm decomposition
and the cache-resident blocking argument — for a seminar audience. Useful as a plainer
route into the ideas; not an independent result, and not a citable origin for any of them.
