Latent Space Communication via K-V Cache Alignment
Lucio M. Dery, Zohar Yahav, Henry Prior, Qixuan Feng, Jiajun Shen, Arthur Szlam
DeepMind / Google
arXiv: 2601.06123
Submitted: January 4, 2026

Abstract:
We propose training lightweight adapter modules that translate a model's internal
key-value cache states into a shared intermediate representation, enabling multiple
language models to share internal states without passing text tokens. The adapters
are optimized per model pair. Experiments use the Gemma-2 model family. Results
show that soft prompts and learned capabilities transfer across model instances
without modifying pre-trained weights.

Key contributions:
- Trained adapter modules for KV cache alignment between model instances
- Shared intermediate representation space for KV states
- Multi-model collaboration improving downstream performance
- Demonstrated on same-family Gemma-2 models
